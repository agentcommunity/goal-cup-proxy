# /goal Cup

PLACEHOLDER: the organisers supply the final text of this skill.

This session is a /goal Cup run, recorded by the Goal Cup tracer (`goalcup`).

**What is recorded.** Everything this harness sends to its model provider and everything it
receives, its own session files, and the commits in the run's repository. The record is sealed
when the run ends and uploaded to the Goal Cup server. Nothing is hidden from it, and nothing in
this session is private.

**What the participant may type.** The participant typed the first prompt. After it they may type
only "keep going", and nothing else: any other message from a human removes the run at review.
The tracer itself never types anything into this session.

**What that means for you.** Nobody is there to answer a question or approve a step. Work on your
own until the goal is done, commit your work to the repository and push it as you go, and stop
when you are finished.
