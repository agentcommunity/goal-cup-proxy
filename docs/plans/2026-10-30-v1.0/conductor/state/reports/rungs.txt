   scripts/pad/pad-run:591-593 kill_recorder: S4 has no open-stream count, so the rig uses "a request in the last 10 s" as a
   stand-in and kills anyway after 60 s. check.py:530-532 then applies PRD-02 R1's "under 2 s" bound (meant for a kill
   while a response streams) to every kill.
   The recorder_down gap starts at the dead boot's last LINK (crates/goalcup-format/src/part.rs:382-425). LINKs are written
   about every second only while records are being written (crates/goalcup/src/pipeline/writer.rs:30-33, 333), and COUNT about
   every 60 s. So a kill in a quiet moment gives a long but honest gap: Claude 3.1 s (last LINK 2.5 s before the kill),
   Codex 31.7 s (last LINK at a COUNT).
   Codex also finishes the smoke prompt in about 45 s, so kill@1 lands after the work and wait-recovery
   (pad-run:600-614, "a new model request") cannot hold.
   Fix choices: for Codex, kill earlier (@0) or use the full prompt; and either judge the bound from the kill time (faults.jsonl
   "at") to the gap end, or give S4 an open-stream count (seam ask to PRD-02). PRD-02 restart itself looks good: the gap ends
   within about 1 s of the kill.
   Small wording issue: pad-run:615 says "recorder 382 up in 51.9 s". That is the time until the next request, not the restart time.
